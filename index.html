Why Chatrix

Built for the way developers share

💻

Code snippets

Wrap code in triple backticks and it shows up with syntax colors and a copy button, in posts, comments and chats.

📊

Polls

Ask your followers a question with 2 to 4 options. Polls run for 1, 3 or 7 days and show results after you vote.

🎬

Video demos

Record or upload a short demo, up to 50 MB. Show a build or a bug fix, and let people like and comment.

⏳

Status updates

Post text or a photo that disappears after 24 hours, 2 days or a week. Perfect for what you're shipping today.

#️⃣

Tags & trending

Type #react and it becomes a link to a page of everything tagged with it. Explore shows what developers are talking about.

🤝

Find collaborators

Filter people by role and tech stack, and switch on “Open to collaborate” so the right people find you.

💬

Chats built for code

Send a snippet in a chat and it arrives formatted, with colors and a copy button. Shift+Enter for new lines; links and #tags work too.

📞

Voice & video calls

Call someone straight from a chat, with mute and camera controls, and see your recent calls in one list.

🧑‍💻

Your dev profile

Photo, banner, tech stack, what you're building now, and links to GitHub, your site and X, plus your follower counts.
